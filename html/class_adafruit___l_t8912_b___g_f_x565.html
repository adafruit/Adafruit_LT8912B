Detailed Description

Adafruit GFX display, separate from the portable I2C bridge driver. Requires Arduino ESP32 3.3.11 or a compatible IDF 5.5 build and PSRAM. The caller owns the bridge and must keep it alive until end(). GFX draws RGB565 colors into framebuffer 0 as BGR888; call show() to flush.
